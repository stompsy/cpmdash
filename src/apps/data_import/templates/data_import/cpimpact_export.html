CP Impact Export

Downloads a de-identified zip of patients, encounters, referrals and OD referrals for import into CP Impact. It's built when you click and sent straight to your browser. Nothing is saved on the server.

What's removed on the way out

  • Addresses, coordinates, ZIP codes, districts and jail dates
  • All dates: rows get a year and quarter instead
  • Sex, race and marital status. Ages become age bands
  • Text values that appear only a few times in a column (in case a name was typed into a category field)

Patients are keyed P + their cpmdash row id. Open codebook.csv in the zip and check it before sharing the file.
